Real-to-sim demos

Each scene is one recorded robot episode. Left of the line is the real robot; right of it, its physics twin in Isaac Sim replays the same recorded actions. Point at a scene to move the line, click it for the full videos, and scroll sideways for more.

How it works

From one recorded demonstration to a physics scene that replays it. The robot arm itself aligns the cameras, and an agent runs each tool and checks the result before moving on. The steps play on their own; drag the scene to look around.

Real-to-sim-to-real

The verified twin also gives a robot agent its context. From the replayed demonstration and the scene's measurements we write a demonstration context: the person's sequence as tool calls, and measured numbers relative to the objects. The agent (ORA) then runs the task twice on new layouts, once with the context and once without it; its model weights never change.

On the real robot

Limitations

Our twins cover rigid objects and simple articulated objects, such as drawers. Soft bodies, such as towels and napkins, and complex articulated objects are left for future work.
